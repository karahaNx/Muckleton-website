The world so far

Five regions, one swamp at a time

Lv 1–6 · Starter

Ribbit Clearing

Twelve frog camps scattered across a quiet clearing. Where every Muckleton character begins.

Lv 6–8 · Cave

Mossback Hollow

A damp descent beneath the clearing, ending in the Broodmother's isolated chamber.

Lv 12–14 · Dungeon

The Sunken Reliquary

Muckleton's first dungeon: a partially flooded temple with sealed gates and a final sanctum.

Lv 7–15 · Outdoor

Mirewood Crossing

Toads, spiders and shamblers along a wooded road, guarded by the Mireheart.

Lv 14–22 · Outdoor

Mirefen Basin

A wide basin of drakes, wisps and drowned cultists, watched over by Ulmor.
