About this wiki

This wiki documents the current alpha build of Muckleton — a 2.5D low-poly isometric MMORPG built solo in Godot. Design inspiration comes from Ragnarok Online and World of Warcraft, but the goal is for Muckleton to earn its own identity rather than copy either.

A few ground rules that shape everything below:

  • Drop info, not drop odds. The Bestiary lists what a creature can reward — gold, materials, gear — never exact percentages. That's a deliberate choice, not a missing feature.
  • "Coming soon" means exactly that. Zones and systems that only exist as a plan are labelled clearly, never presented as playable.
  • No accounts yet. Character progress lives on your own PC for now. See the Roadmap for what changes that.

World & zones

Muckleton's regions unlock roughly in this order. Each outdoor zone connects to the next by a continuous route rather than a loading-screen jump.

Ribbit Clearing Lv 1–6

The starter zone. Twelve named Frog camps hold a total of 58 normal Frogs, ranging from beginner camps near spawn to tougher pockets further out. Home to the first quest hub and the entrance to Mossback Hollow.

Mossback Hollow Lv 6–8 · Cave

A cave beneath the clearing: fourteen Mossback Frogs guarding a narrow descent, ending in the Broodmother's own isolated chamber.

The Sunken Reliquary Lv 12–14 · Dungeon

Muckleton's first proper dungeon — a partially flooded temple beneath Ribbit Valley. Eight elite camps guard the route to two environmental seals; both must be activated before the final sanctum opens.

Mirewood Crossing Lv 7–15 · Outdoor

The road north out of the clearing, populated by Bog Toads, Mirefang Spiders and Rotcap Shamblers across nine grind camps, guarded by the isolated Mireheart. Two side dungeons — Rotroot Burrow and Briarfen Hold — branch off the main road.

Mirefen Basin Lv 14–22 · Outdoor

A wide basin built around a Fenwarden hub, with five distinct monster families — Fen Drakes, Marsh Wisps, Reed Stalkers, Drowned Acolytes and a Reedspine Dartcaller pocket — spread across thirteen camps. Ulmor, the Basin Horror, holds the zone's outdoor boss encounter. Three more instances branch from here: Gloamroot Cave, The Drowned Temple and Rootvault Depths.

On the horizon Not yet playable

Systems

Combat

Right-click to Auto Attack. Melee classes close to melee range; Hunter and Mage approach to 3 tiles and fire from range. Hotbar slot 1 holds each class's signature ability on its own cooldown.

Leveling & XP

Levels 1–60. XP is awarded once per legitimate kill, shared automatically across a party. Max HP grows with every level.

Talent trees

Every class has a full tree: a Foundation tier, a deeper Lower Tree, Keystones, and mutually exclusive 2nd/3rd Class capstones.

Inventory & equipment

A 48-slot bag with auto-sort and category filters, plus 15 equipment slots. Right-click a bag item to equip it.

Rest

Sit down with X to recover — cancelled by almost any gameplay input or incoming damage.

Social

Zone and whisper chat, a mutual-accept friends list, and party support with shared XP.

Dungeons

Instanced runs with party ownership, objective tracking, and a wipe counter. Defeating the final boss completes the run.

Character saves

Currently stored locally per PC — level, XP, position, talents, inventory, gold, equipment, quests, friends and appearance all persist between sessions on the same machine.
