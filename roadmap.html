Roadmap

What's live, what's next, what's planned

Built by one developer, in public. This page reflects priority, not promised dates — and nothing here is marked "Now" unless it's actually playable today.

Now — playable

Current alpha

  • 5 classes — Warrior, Rogue, Hunter, Mage, Luminary — each with a full talent tree.
  • 5 regions — Ribbit Clearing, Mossback Hollow, The Sunken Reliquary, Mirewood Crossing, Mirefen Basin, plus five side instances.
  • Inventory & equipment — 48-slot bag, 15 gear slots, drops from kills.
  • Party, friends & chat — zone/whisper channels, mutual-accept friends.
  • Local character saves — persistent on your own PC, not yet account-bound.
Next — Road to Level 30

World foundation is laid; content is next

  • Gloamcap Wilds — fungal melee/ranged camps.
  • Ashenroot Frontier — drakes, crushers, ember casters.
  • Threshold March — a focused elite gauntlet leading to Muckleton's first raid, The Crownless Gate.
  • Elderhavn — the capital city: Market Exchange, Forge & Repairs, Training Court, Guild Quarter, Wayfarer Shrine.
Later — planned

Not started yet

  • Persistent accounts — server-authoritative saves so a character isn't tied to one PC. This is also what a future website login would connect to.
  • Trading & vendors — currently no economy between players.
  • Full item system — affixes, item level, durability, bind rules.
  • Mounts and additional class specializations.
  • Community accounts on this site — depends entirely on the backend above existing first.

Why no dates? Muckleton is built by one person outside of a studio schedule. A date I can't guarantee isn't useful to you — the order above is. Follow the devlog for what actually shipped, when it shipped.
