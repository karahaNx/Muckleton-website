Recently in the swamp

Latest

Mirefen Basin opens up

The swamp keeps going. Mirefen Basin is the third outdoor region, built around a Fenwarden hub and populated by five distinct monster families instead of another re-skinned Frog camp — Fen Drakes, Marsh Wisps, Reed Stalkers, Drowned Acolytes, and a Reedspine Dartcaller pocket. The zone's own boss, Ulmor the Basin Horror, waits at the far end. Three more instances branch off from here for later exploration.

Previous

The Sunken Reliquary — Muckleton's first dungeon

An old, partially flooded temple beneath Ribbit Valley. Eight elite camps stand between the entrance and two environmental seals — both have to be activated before the deep wing opens up. Sluice Guardian holds the miniboss room; the Mirebound Warden waits at the end.

Previous

Luminary joins the roster

A fifth playable class: healer-first, closer to a Shadow Priest than "a Mage with heals." Luminous Brand heals the caster on a successful hit, and Luminary carries enough ranged damage on its own to level solo when a group isn't around.

Earlier

Talent trees, fully authored

All five classes now have complete trees — a Foundation tier, a deeper Lower Tree, Keystones, and mutually exclusive 2nd/3rd Class capstones. No more placeholder branches.

Have feedback right now? The Discord is the fastest way to reach the dev directly — that's genuinely where alpha feedback shapes what gets built next.
